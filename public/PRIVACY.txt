Jungle didakoo - Privacy notice

No sign-up, no cookies, no analytics. The app keeps in your browser only what it needs to work:
your guest and session keys, the BOT match in progress and your guest wins.

Online matches:
PRIVATE and ARENA matches connect to the Streamr peer-to-peer network: other nodes of the network
can see your public IP address.

On-chain matches:
Matches between wallets are recorded on Polygon: wallet address, moves, result and match token are
public and permanent, and cannot be deleted.

Third-party services:
To work, the app contacts services that can see your public IP address:
- blockchain providers: Tenderly, thirdweb, dRPC, PublicNode, QuickNode, The Graph; as in any
  blockchain app, they receive the app's requests, including the address of the wallet you play
  with (MetaMask or Farcaster);
- network services used by Streamr: Fastly, Cloudflare, CloudFront;
- GitHub, for version announcements;
- the host serving this page.
MetaMask and Farcaster, if you use them, have their own privacy policies.
